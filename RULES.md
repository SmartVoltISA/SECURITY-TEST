# Rules

1. Test only assets and techniques explicitly authorized by the target program.
2. Read the current program policy before testing; scope and exclusions take precedence over this repository.
3. Do not access, retain, publish, or expose unrelated personal or confidential data.
4. Do not perform destructive actions, denial of service, persistence, malware deployment, credential theft, or irreversible state changes.
5. Stop once the minimum evidence needed to demonstrate impact has been obtained.
6. Keep credentials, secrets, tokens, private customer data, and other sensitive material out of this public repository.
7. Redact evidence before publication.
8. Record uncertainty honestly. A hypothesis is not a finding until validated.
9. Do not reuse a finding outside the authorized target or scope.
10. For public case files, describe the security mechanism and evidence without publishing unnecessary operational exploit instructions.
11. Guardian receives defensive abstractions and regression tests, not unrestricted offensive capability.
12. Monetary rewards are external outcomes and must never be treated as guaranteed.
